# 📦 Retail Inventory Analytics & Demand Forecasting Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Inventory Analytics & Demand Forecasting Dashboard** is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to help businesses monitor inventory, analyze product demand, identify stock risks, and make better replenishment decisions.

The project combines historical sales data, product information, demand forecasting, inventory valuation, ABC analysis, safety stock, reorder points, and EOQ calculations into one interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses need to maintain the right amount of inventory.

Too much inventory can lead to:

- 💰 Higher holding costs
- 📦 Overstocking
- 🏪 Capital being blocked in unsold products

Too little inventory can lead to:

- ⚠️ Stockouts
- 📉 Lost sales
- 😞 Poor customer experience

This project uses historical sales and inventory data to provide insights that support better inventory planning.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important inventory KPIs:

- Total Products
- Total Inventory Value
- Products At Risk
- A-Class Products
- Average Days of Stock

### 🔹 Demand Analysis

- Average Monthly Demand
- Next-Month Forecast
- Product-wise demand comparison

### 🔹 Inventory Analysis

- Current inventory value
- Stock health
- Days of stock remaining
- Reorder status

### 🔹 ABC Analysis

Products are classified into:

- **A Class** – High-value products
- **B Class** – Medium-value products
- **C Class** – Lower-value products

### 🔹 Inventory Risk Analysis

The dashboard identifies products that may require attention using:

- Safety Stock
- Reorder Point
- Current Stock
- Days of Stock Remaining
- At-Risk Status

### 🔹 Top Products

The dashboard highlights the:

**Top 5 Products by Inventory Value**
